# ROCKSOUL

**RIOT CREATIFY. MAKE INTELLIGENCE MOVE.**

Rocksoul is a desktop creative-intelligence workbench for people who refuse to leave ideas trapped in chat windows. It turns intent into research, plans, prototypes, software, media, and verified work through human-directed agents and tools.

> **Not another chatbot. A workshop for ideas that refuse to stay ideas.**

## The thesis

AI is not obsolete. The chatbot-shaped version of AI is too small for the next chapter.

Rocksoul explores a wider interaction model: an intelligence workspace that can hold project context, investigate a question, plan a sequence, use approved tools, build tangible artifacts, test its own output, and show its evidence. The human stays in charge of purpose, permissions, judgment, and release.

**AGI is the north star, not a product claim.** Rocksoul is an AGI-oriented research and creation platform. It must never describe itself as having achieved Artificial General Intelligence unless that claim is independently demonstrated against explicit, published evaluation criteria.

## Mission

Make powerful creative and technical work accessible without surrendering human agency, privacy, authorship, or accountability.

## What Rocksoul is

- A personal intelligence workbench, not a talking mascot.
- A creative foundry for research, writing, code, design, media, and experiments.
- A human-directed agent environment with visible context, tool permissions, plans, evidence, and checkpoints.
- A desktop-first workspace that can preserve local work and remain useful when disconnected.
- A long-term platform for studying increasingly general capabilities through measurable evaluations.

## What Rocksoul is not

- Not a claim that AGI already exists.
- Not an autonomous authority over people, money, accounts, infrastructure, or physical systems.
- Not a replacement for human responsibility, expertise, relationships, or conscience.
- Not a wrapper that simply sends every prompt to one model and calls the result intelligence.
- Not a black box that hides sources, actions, uncertainty, or failures.

## The creative loop

1. **SPARK** — capture a raw thought, problem, sketch, link, voice note, or brief.
2. **RIFF** — shape it into a project with goals, context, constraints, and references.
3. **THE PIT** — assemble specialist agents and tools around a bounded mission.
4. **FORGE** — produce the artifact: research dossier, code, design, prototype, plan, or media.
5. **SOUND CHECK** — test claims, run checks, inspect provenance, and expose uncertainty.
6. **STAGE** — approve, export, publish, or hand the work to another person.

The loop is deliberately observable. Each stage should show what came in, what happened, what changed, what evidence exists, and what still needs a human decision.

## First product slice

The first release should do a few things exceptionally well:

- A project-centered home with recent work, active missions, and a clear local/online state.
- A canvas where briefs, sources, notes, artifacts, and decisions live together.
- A mission runner that proposes a plan before it executes tools.
- A small, permissioned tool belt for local files, repository inspection, search, and approved commands.
- An artifact shelf with versions, diffs, provenance, and export.
- A review lane with source checks, tests, quality gates, and human approval.
- A local-first project store with explicit backup and sync controls.

Do not block this slice on a marketplace, unrestricted autonomous execution, an elaborate multi-agent simulation, or a promise of universal intelligence.

## Product principles

**Human intent is the root permission.** Agents can propose actions; the user grants capabilities. Destructive, external, financial, identity, publication, and infrastructure actions require explicit confirmation.

**Evidence beats confidence.** Show sources, test output, tool traces, assumptions, and uncertainty. Never turn a fluent answer into a false claim of verification.

**Local-first means durable.** Work must survive app restarts and offline sessions. Clearly distinguish local saved, queued for sync, synced, conflicted, and failed.

**The system can disagree.** Specialist agents should challenge assumptions and surface contradictions rather than manufacture consensus.

**Capability must be measurable.** AGI-oriented progress is tracked through transparent evaluations: generalization, tool reliability, long-horizon task completion, calibration, recovery, and safety—not marketing adjectives.

**The soul stays human.** Technology is an instrument. It must not become an idol, an unaccountable ruler, or a substitute for human dignity.

## Relationship to the Sovereign ecosystem

Rocksoul is a new product surface under apps/rocksoul. It may reuse proven shared packages and authenticated backend services, but it must not inherit unfinished desktop mock data or assume that existing service integrations are production-ready. Every reused capability needs a real contract, permission boundary, and test.

## Current status

This directory begins as a product and brand foundation. It is not yet a runnable desktop application. Implementation should begin only after the product boundary, first user journey, data model, and native security model are agreed and testable.
